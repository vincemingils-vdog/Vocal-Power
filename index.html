Vocal Pitch & Projection Arcade

A real-time classroom singing game powered by your microphone. Here is how to get started:

  • 1. Activate Microphone & Permissions Click Start Microphone in the top-right corner and select Allow when your browser asks for mic access.
  • 2. Pick & Play Your Target Pitch Choose a note from the Target Pitch dropdown and click ♫ Hear Pitch to play a reference tone for the room. Keep Octave-Free Mode on so changed and unchanged voices match in any octave.
  • 3. Calibrate for Your Room Use the one-touch Warm-Up / Section Battle / Boss Mode presets, or adjust the Volume Floor and Pitch Tolerance sliders to match your ensemble size.
  • 4. Intonation History & Dynamics Watch the Pitch Ribbon in the drawer to see if singers drive sharp or flat during long tones. In Mode 4 (Paper Glider), volume directly controls glider altitude!
